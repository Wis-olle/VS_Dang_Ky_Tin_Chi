This file summarizes the official license; it is not a substitute for the
source document. The twelve stock photographs listed in
Unsplash-Avatar-Sources.json were selected from free-download photo pages,
not Unsplash+ paid content.

The Unsplash License allows free downloading, copying, modification,
distribution and use of images, including commercial and non-commercial
use. Attribution is appreciated and the app preserves photographer credits
in docs/ASSET_SOURCES.md and Unsplash-Avatar-Sources.json.

The license does not permit selling images without significant modification
or compiling Unsplash images to reproduce a similar or competing service.
The app downloads images directly from the public image CDN without using
an Unsplash API integration. The images are bundled locally and serve only
as stock illustrations for fictional lecturer records.

All photographer names, original photo-page URLs, CDN crop URLs, dimensions
and verification dates are recorded in Unsplash-Avatar-Sources.json.
