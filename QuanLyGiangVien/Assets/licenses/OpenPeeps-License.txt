Open Peeps and DiceBear both identify this artwork as CC0. The original
library permits personal and commercial use, copying, remixing and changing
colors. The avatars do not depict the fictional lecturers in the demo data.
Author and source credit are retained as project documentation.

The 12 images are deliberately curated with different head shapes, hair,
expressions, glasses, and facial hair. Native DiceBear colors produce muted
mint, cyan, peach, lilac, cream and gray details. Source SVG geometry is
unchanged; Sharp/librsvg exports the SVG as a local 512 x 512 RGBA PNG.
The desktop application loads these local files without an internet request.

OpenPeeps-Avatar-Sources.json records the exact API URL, all option values,
source SVG checksum and exported PNG checksum for each avatar. The matching
SVG source is stored beside its PNG in Assets/avatars.
OpenPeeps-Avatar-ContactSheet.jpg is the visual review sheet.
